Workplace Mindfulness Assessment

A short, honest self-check for the HR function — from individual contributors to the CHRO. Ten questions in total: a couple about your day-to-day work, two quick logical-reasoning questions (all in your head, no paper needed, and no right or wrong answer), and the rest simple, everyday questions about how present you generally are. At the end you'll get a mindfulness score out of 30.
